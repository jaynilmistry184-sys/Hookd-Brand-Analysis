📊 HOOKd: D2C Executive Growth & Revenue Dashboard
📝 Project Overview
This project features a comprehensive, dark-theme Executive Growth and Revenue Dashboard designed for HOOKd, India's first high-protein baked chicken chips brand.
In the fast-paced Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) and FMCG space, founders often suffer from "data silos"—checking Shopify for D2C sales, Meta for ad spend, and Zepto/Blinkit for quick-commerce volume. This dashboard unifies these disparate data streams into a single, interactive pane of glass, enabling rapid diagnostic analysis and data-driven scaling decisions.
🎯 Core Objectives
1.	Unify Channel Data: Map performance across D2C (Shopify), Quick-Commerce (Zepto, Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart), and Offline Retail.
2.	Track Margin Erosion: Differentiate between Gross Revenue and Net Revenue to identify where heavy promotional discounting is hurting profitability.
3.	Optimize Marketing Spend: Correlate campaign spend with actual clicks and attributed revenue to find the most efficient Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).
4.	Monitor Operational Health: Track delivery success rates versus return/cancellation leaks.
📈 Key Visualizations & Features
•	Channel Contribution (Treemap): Visualizes Net Revenue by Sales Channel, instantly highlighting the volume reliance on Offline Retail and Shopify versus the emerging Quick-Commerce platforms.
•	Marketing Attribution (Scatter Plot & Bar Charts): Cross-references Gross Sales by Campaign Type with a scatter plot of Spend vs. Clicks. This isolates high-performing organic/creator campaigns from bloated paid social efforts.
•	Geographic Velocity (Bar Chart): Maps Gross Revenue by City, confirming Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore as Tier-1 anchor markets requiring priority inventory routing.
•	Fulfillment Diagnostics (Donut Chart): Tracks order delivery statuses, flagging the ~4% return/cancellation rate for targeted logistics auditing.
•	Temporal Trends (Line Chart): Maps Month-over-Month (MoM) revenue growth to identify seasonal spikes and baseline velocity.
•	Dynamic SKU Slicing: A global filter allowing executives to instantly drill down into specific flavor profiles (e.g., Tandoori Tikka vs. Peri Peri) across all metrics.
💡 Strategic Business Value & Solutions
By leveraging this dashboard, the brand can execute the following strategic pivots:
1.	Targeted Inventory Routing: By identifying exactly which cities (Mumbai/Delhi) and which SKUs drive the most velocity, operations can route inventory to specific dark stores to prevent costly stockouts.
2.	Ad-Spend Reallocation: The marketing ROI visuals allow the growth team to cut funding from low-yield campaigns (high spend/low clicks) and double down on highly efficient "Creator Codes" and localized bundles.
3.	Channel-Specific Margin Control: Highlighting Net Revenue reveals the hidden costs of Quick-Commerce commissions, allowing the brand to balance high-volume Q-commerce acquisition with high-margin Shopify retention.
🛠️ Tech Stack & Implementation
•	Tool: Power BI
•	Data Processing: Power Query, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for custom KPIs and margin calculations.
•	Dataset: Simulated 10,000+ row dataset mimicking real-world D2C transaction volumes, quick-commerce splits, and marketing unit economics.
•	UI/UX: Custom dark-mode executive theme designed for high contrast, readability, and immediate cognitive focus on critical alerts.
